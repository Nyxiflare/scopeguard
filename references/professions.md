# What usually counts as extra, by profession

Use these lists two ways: to fill the baseline's "silent on" list, and to spot grey areas when a
request arrives. They describe common practice, not rules. The user's agreement always wins.

## Graphic and brand design
- Extra concepts beyond the agreed number; a new direction after a concept was chosen
- Extra formats: business cards, letterhead, social profile and banner sizes, favicon, merch mockups
- Brand guidelines or a style guide
- Source files (AI/PSD/Figma) when only exports were agreed
- Animated logo versions
- Font or stock image licences
- Commonly silent on: source file ownership, social media sizes, print vs. digital versions

## Web design and development
- Pages beyond the agreed count; a blog, shop, booking or membership system added later
- Content writing, image sourcing, product uploads
- New integrations (payment, CRM, email tool, analytics) not listed
- Browser/device support beyond what was agreed; accessibility audits
- Migration from an old site; hosting setup; domain and email setup
- Ongoing maintenance, updates, backups after launch
- Bugs in **your own code** before launch are included. Changes in requirements are not.
- Commonly silent on: who writes the content, post-launch support period, number of templates vs. pages

## Video editing and motion
- Extra versions: new aspect ratios (9:16, 1:1), cut-downs, different lengths
- Added runtime, new scenes, new graphics or animations
- Captions/subtitles, translations, extra music licensing
- Re-edits after a cut was approved and locked
- Colour grading or sound design beyond basic
- Raw footage handover or project files
- Commonly silent on: number of versions, caption files, music licence, project file handover

## Writing and copywriting
- Extra pieces, word count over the agreed range, new topics
- Research interviews, SEO keyword research, meta descriptions
- Uploading to the CMS, formatting, sourcing images
- Rewrites after a brief changes (as opposed to edits on the agreed brief)
- Commonly silent on: number of edit rounds, who uploads, SEO extras

## Social media and marketing
- Extra platforms, extra posts per week, stories/reels on top of feed posts
- Community management (replying to comments/DMs), ad spend management
- Creating assets when only scheduling was agreed
- Monthly reports or calls beyond what was agreed
- Commonly silent on: response time for comments, who supplies photos, number of report calls

## UGC and content creators (brand deals)
- Extra videos, extra hooks or variations, raw footage
- Usage rights beyond the agreed period, platform or use as paid ads ("whitelisting"/"Spark ads")
- Exclusivity (not working with competitors) beyond what was agreed
- Re-shoots for reasons outside the brief
- Posting on extra platforms; link in bio for longer than agreed
- Commonly silent on: usage period, paid-ads rights, exclusivity, number of revisions

## Photography
- Extra edited images beyond the package; retouching beyond basic
- Extra hours on site, extra locations, travel
- RAW files; commercial usage rights
- Rush delivery
- Commonly silent on: number of edited images, RAW files, usage rights

## Consulting and coaching
- Extra calls or workshops, longer sessions
- Implementation work when only advice was agreed
- Async support (unlimited messages/email) outside sessions
- Extra stakeholders or teams joining
- Commonly silent on: messaging between sessions, prep time, number of attendees

## Apps and software products
- New features or screens; changes to agreed user flows
- Extra platforms (iOS + Android + web), app-store submission
- Admin panels, analytics dashboards
- Third-party API changes outside the developer's control
- Commonly silent on: app-store work, post-launch fixes window, design responsibility
