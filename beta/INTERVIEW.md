# Exit interview (20 minutes)

Record it only with permission, or take notes. Ask about **what they did**, not what they think they'd do. Don't defend the product: just listen, and ask "tell me more".

## Warm-up (2 min)
1. In one sentence, how did the two weeks go?

## Setup (3 min)
2. Walk me through installing it. Where did you hesitate or get stuck?
3. Did the scope summary it built from your agreement look right? What did you have to correct?

## Real use (8 min)
4. Tell me about the request where ScopeGuard was **most** useful. What happened?
5. Tell me about a time it was **wrong** or you disagreed. What did it say, and what was right?
6. Did you ever feel it was trying to charge for something the client had a fair claim to? *(Watch for false extras. This is the most important question.)*
7. Did you send its reply drafts? As-is or edited? What did you change, and why?
8. How did clients react to the replies or change orders?
9. Did you trust its money totals? Did you check any yourself?

## Value (4 min)
10. Over the two weeks, roughly how much extra work got **paid** that you'd normally have done free?
11. What would you go back to if ScopeGuard disappeared tomorrow?
12. **Would you pay to keep using it?** If yes: what's the most you'd pay per month before it feels too expensive? And what price would feel like a bargain?
    *(Then, if they gave a price: "If it were [their price] today, would you sign up now?" Note yes / no / hesitation.)*
13. Who else do you know who'd want this?

## Wrap-up (3 min)
14. If you could change one thing, what would it be?
15. Anything I didn't ask that I should have?
16. May I quote you (anonymously, or by first name and field) on the website? *(Yes / anonymous only / no)*
