# Beta application form

Paste these questions into Google Forms, Tally or Typeform. It takes about 3 minutes to fill in. Questions marked ★ are deal-breakers.

**Intro text for the form:**
> ScopeGuard checks client requests against your agreement and tells you what's included, what's extra and what to charge. We're picking 5 freelancers from different fields to test it on a real project for 2 weeks. Your client data stays on your computer. This is not legal advice.

## About you
1. First name (or what you'd like to be called)
2. Contact: email, or Discord / LinkedIn handle
3. ★ What do you mainly do? *(one choice)* Graphic/brand design · Video editing · Web development · Social media management · Copywriting · Other: ___
4. How long have you freelanced? *(<1 year · 1–3 years · 3–5 years · 5+ years)*
5. Roughly how many client projects do you run at once? *(1 · 2–3 · 4–6 · 7+)*

## The problem
6. In the last 3 months, how often did a client ask for work you hadn't agreed to? *(Never · Once or twice · Monthly · Weekly · On most projects)*
7. What did you usually do? *(Did it free · Charged extra · Pushed back · Depends)*
8. Roughly how much unpaid extra work did you do last quarter? *(None · <10 hours · 10–30 hours · 30+ hours · No idea)*
9. What do you use today to keep track of agreed scope and changes? *(Nothing · My memory · Notes/docs · Spreadsheet · Project tool: ___ · Other)*

## Can you test? ★
10. ★ Do you have Claude Code, or a paid Claude plan and willingness to install Claude Code? *(Yes, already use it · Yes, will install · No)*
11. How comfortable are you running a command in a terminal? *(Never done it · A little · Comfortable)*
12. ★ Do you have an active project with a written agreement (contract, proposal, quote or email)? *(Yes · Starting one in the next week · No)*
13. ★ Do you expect at least one new client request on it in the next 2 weeks? *(Yes · Maybe · No)*
14. Which operating system? *(Windows · macOS · Linux)*

## Consent
15. ★ I'll remove client names, emails and personal details from anything I share. *(Agree)*
16. May anonymised examples from your project become test cases that make ScopeGuard more accurate? *(Yes · Ask me per example · No)* (Not a deal-breaker.)
17. Okay to do a 20-minute call at the end? *(Yes · Prefer a written questionnaire)*

## Picking your 5
- Must pass every ★ question.
- Cover all 5 fields, and mix terminal comfort levels: you want at least one "never done it" person to find onboarding problems.
- Prefer people who answered "Monthly" or more on question 6 and chose "Did it free" on question 7. They're the people the product is for.
- Keep 2 backups. Usually 1–2 testers drop out.
